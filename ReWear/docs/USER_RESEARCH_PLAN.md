# Closet Cycle user research plan and evidence record

## Status

No interviews or usability sessions are recorded in this repository. Do not present the questions or target sample below as completed research. The team must collect consent and enter real, anonymized observations before claiming user validation.

## Research questions

1. Do campus members have wearable clothing they want to pass on?
2. What makes a pre-owned clothing exchange feel useful and trustworthy?
3. Which item details, condition evidence, sizing information, and filters matter most?
4. Would members prefer direct swaps, internal credits, or neither? Why?
5. What meetup method feels safe and practical? What would make a member cancel?
6. What would stop members from listing or requesting an item?

## Suggested method

- Recruit 10–15 volunteers from the intended campus group, including potential listers and requesters. This is a suggested sample, not a completed sample.
- Explain the class-project purpose, ask permission before recording notes, avoid collecting sensitive personal data, and let participants skip questions or stop.
- Ask open questions about recent behavior before showing the prototype. Avoid leading questions and asking participants to endorse the idea.
- Follow with a short task-based prototype session: find an item, inspect details, create a listing, start an exchange, and find safety/report guidance.
- Record anonymized notes, task outcomes, recurring themes, and quotes only with explicit permission.

## Interview guide

1. Tell me about the last time you wanted clothing you did not already own.
2. What did you do with clothing you no longer wear? What made that easy or difficult?
3. Have you bought, sold, borrowed, or swapped second-hand clothes? What happened?
4. What information would you need before requesting a listed item?
5. How would you decide between a direct swap and a credit-based exchange?
6. What concerns would you have about meeting another member to exchange an item?
7. What would make you trust or avoid a campus exchange service?
8. After the prototype task, what was confusing, slow, or missing?
9. What would you change first? What would make you use it again?

DOMAIN CLASSES: MODEL PACKAGE

AuditedEntity is abstract and has id: Long, createdAt: Instant, updatedAt: Instant. UserAccount extends AuditedEntity and includes displayName, email, area, creditBalance, passwordHash, role: UserRole. ClothingItem extends AuditedEntity and includes title, description, category, size, conditionLabel, color, brand, creditValue, imageUrl, status: ItemStatus, owner: UserAccount. ExchangeRequest extends AuditedEntity and includes requester: UserAccount, owner: UserAccount, requestedItem: ClothingItem, offeredItem: ClothingItem (optional), type: ExchangeType, status: ExchangeStatus, creditDifference, requesterConfirmed, ownerConfirmed. CreditTransaction extends AuditedEntity and includes user: UserAccount, exchangeRequest: ExchangeRequest (optional), amount, description. MemberReport extends AuditedEntity and includes reporter: UserAccount, item: ClothingItem, reason, details, status.
Enumerations: UserRole {MEMBER, MODERATOR}; ItemStatus {AVAILABLE, RESERVED, EXCHANGED, ARCHIVED}; ExchangeType {DIRECT_SWAP, CREDITS}; ExchangeStatus {REQUESTED, ACCEPTED, ARRANGED, COMPLETED, REJECTED, CANCELLED}. Show inheritance from AuditedEntity to all five entity classes. Show associations and multiplicities: one user owns zero-to-many ClothingItems; a user can be requester for zero-to-many ExchangeRequests and owner for zero-to-many ExchangeRequests; each exchange has one requester, one owner, one requested item, and zero or one offered item; one user has zero-to-many CreditTransactions; an exchange may be referenced by zero-to-many credit ledger entries; one user files zero-to-many MemberReports; one clothing item receives zero-to-many reports.
